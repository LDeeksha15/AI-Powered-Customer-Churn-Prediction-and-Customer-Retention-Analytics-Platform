CHURN PREDICTION

Predict Customer Churn

Enter the customer's RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) values to generate a live churn risk prediction.

Try an example:
Frequency (F): the total number of distinct orders a customer has placed. Higher frequency generally signals stronger engagement.
Monetary (M): the customer's total lifetime spend across all orders. A core input for both churn risk and CLV.
Total spent divided by number of orders. Helps distinguish frequent-small-basket shoppers from occasional-big-basket ones.
Recency context: days since the customer's very first purchase. Combined with low frequency, long tenure can signal a fading relationship.
